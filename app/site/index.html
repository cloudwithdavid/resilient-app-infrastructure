/About

I'm building toward cloud engineering with a strong foundation in AWS, Linux, and networking. My work spans infrastructure deployment & operations, automation, and applied AI, with an emphasis on architectural decisions and system behavior.

My strong interest in technology is understanding how complete systems fit together. I'm naturally drawn to following the relationships between components, including how information flows, what each component depends on, and how everything behaves when something goes wrong. That inclination, alongside my fascination with intelligent, autonomous systems and conviction in their transformative potential, is what draws me toward building cloud and AI solutions.
